# Level 02 challenges · Networking

## Recall round (+10 XP each)
Answer out loud or in writing **without notes**. Tick only when you can explain it in under 2 minutes.

1. [ ] TCP vs UDP: when would you choose UDP for a real product feature?
2. [ ] What happens, step by step, when you type `https://example.com` and press Enter?
3. [ ] How does DNS resolution work, and what is the trade-off of a long vs short TTL?
4. [ ] Forward proxy vs reverse proxy: who does each one protect or hide?
5. [ ] What did HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 fix over HTTP/1.1?

## Boss fight (+50 XP)
> Trace a request that loads a video thumbnail on a phone in India from a server in the US. Mark every hop (DNS, TCP, TLS, CDN, origin) and estimate where the milliseconds go. Then cut total latency in half.

Rules: 30–45 minutes, draw it in Excalidraw, save your answer as `boss-fight.md` (and the diagram) in this folder. Then ask Claude: *"Act as a senior interviewer. Here is my design for … Grade it 1–10 and tell me what's missing."*
