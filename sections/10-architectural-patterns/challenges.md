# Level 10 challenges · Architectural Patterns

## Recall round (+10 XP each)
Answer out loud or in writing **without notes**. Tick only when you can explain it in under 2 minutes.

1. [ ] When is a monolith the right answer? What signals tell you to split it?
2. [ ] What is CQRS, and what new problem does it create?
3. [ ] Event sourcing: how do you get current state, and how do you handle schema changes in old events?
4. [ ] Event-driven architecture: what gets easier and what gets harder to debug?
5. [ ] Serverless: what are cold starts and when do they matter?
